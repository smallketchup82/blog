Hey fellow internet strangers, It's been a couple of months since my last blog post, so I figured it's time for another one. This blog post is going to be largely documenting how I found my passion for technology, the sacrifices I made along the way, and also tying up some loose ends. Let's begin!

## osu!
I want to give a quick update on where I am in relation with osu! development. Since the last blog post, I've basically ceased active development on osu!(lazer). My primary reason is that the core osu! development team seems to be swamped in PR's, having gone as far as to literally block PR's from outside contributors for a week to avoid more work getting stacked up for them.

This puts me in an unusual position. I've always been the type of lazer contributor that valued infrequent, yet truly groundbreaking PR's. I was the type of person who read the GitHub Discussion threads, found people who were asking for complex quality of life changes, and then I'd go, spend weeks/months, and then PR it.

But this is a fundamental disconnect with what the lazer project *needs*. The core team doesn't need an external contributor that's throwing a bunch of mediocre quality, yet high complexity changes at them every month. The time for big modifications to lazer from external contributors was back in the early 2020s. Right now the project is matured, and quality of life changes are inherently low priority. The core team prefers to handle high complexity changes on their own.

That's not to say that I'm angry, frustrated, or upset at the core team. I understand their point of view, yet I don't want to keep contributing to a project where my pull requests can take years to get reviewed, its just not a productive use of time. And it's not productive for the core team either.

I'll likely only be working to get my current pull requests merged, once the core team can spare their attention to review them. But I don't see myself making any new contributions to osu!(lazer) for the near future. I'll let the osu!(lazer) team sort out the current pull requests, get their backlog reduced, merge my current pull requests, before I decide to return and begin adding to their workload again.

TL;DR: I've come to realize that the project has more external contributors than the core team can actually manage, they're getting bombarded with enough PR's as-is, and so my skills aren't really needed nor wanted at the moment. I'll be taking an indefinite break from osu! development as a result.
